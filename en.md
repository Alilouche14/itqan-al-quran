# Itqan al Quran Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Itqan al Quran (technical identifier: com.alilouche.itqanalquran) is a free app that helps you memorize the Quran, with no ads, no account and no analytics. This page explains, plainly, what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it.

## In short
- Your data stays on your phone. The app sends it to no one.
- One exception, for voice checking: on some phones, the audio of your recitation is sent to Google to be transcribed (see "Voice and speech recognition").
- No ads, no tracking, no account.

## Data stored on your phone
This data is kept in the app's private storage and never leaves the device:
- your first name and profile photo, if you choose them (the photo is picked from your gallery and copied locally);
- your progress, practice sessions, statistics and program;
- your settings (language, theme, reciter, text size);
- your recitation recordings, in the app's internal storage. The app never uploads them.

Retention: as long as you keep the app. Deletion: the trash in My recordings and My progress deletes the items you choose; clearing the app's data (Android settings) or uninstalling it deletes everything. The backup feature creates a file that you control; the app does not upload it.

## Microphone
The microphone is used only when you start a practice or a recording. A recording can continue with the screen off, with a visible notification, until you stop it. The microphone is never turned on without your knowledge. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

## Voice and speech recognition
To check your recitation, Itqan al Quran uses Android speech recognition (SpeechRecognizer). It depends on your phone:
- if the engine installed on the device supports Arabic, transcription can happen without a network;
- otherwise (the case on some models), Android switches to Google's online service: the audio of your recitation is then sent to Google to be transcribed. This processing is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Itqan al Quran has no control over that service and receives only the recognized text, processed on your phone.
If you do not want this sharing, do not start voice checking: the rest of the app works without it. To erase what Google may have kept, use your Google account (myactivity.google.com) and the phone's voice settings.

## Network access
The internet is used to:
- download recitation audio from EveryAyah (everyayah.com): like any download, these requests reveal your IP address to that service; no app identifier is attached;
- online speech recognition as described above, depending on your phone.

## External links
The Institutes page offers links (Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, website, phone, Google Maps). They open in another app only if you tap them. Their privacy is then governed by those services.

## Notifications
Reminders are created on your phone, without a server. You can turn them off in the app or in Android.

## No tracking, no ads, no account
Itqan al Quran contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting tools and no advertising ID. No account is created. We do not sell or share data.

## Content sources
Quran text: Tanzil (tanzil.net, CC BY 3.0, unmodified). Page fonts: Quran Foundation. Audio: EveryAyah. Translations and hadiths: published sources, credited in the app.

## Children
Itqan al Quran is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. A parent who lets a younger child use it should know that voice checking may send their voice to Google (see above) and may choose not to enable it.

## Your rights
Since your data is on your phone, you can view and delete it yourself. For any question or request, write to us.
